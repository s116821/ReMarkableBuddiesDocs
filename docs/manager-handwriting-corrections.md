fresh single-head preparation, separate once-only removal from active use and
original-operation status through the delivered offline producer. Historical
text, samples and backups remain. An unknown original submission remains distinct
from a later observed historical tombstone; neither supplies automatic retry or
permanent current-state authority.

custody. The active [consumer contract](../openspec/changes/handwriting-record-management/contract.md)
and [public projection](../openspec/changes/handwriting-record-management/contract-public.md)
remain central. No sample display, physical erasure, recognition efficacy,
provider/native acceptance or whole Manager/handwriting/1.0 completion follows.
