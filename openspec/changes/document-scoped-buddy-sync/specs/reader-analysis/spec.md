### Requirement: Installed selected Reader prepares exact historical input

The selected resident Reader SHALL admit both verified registered pure
Conversation and mixed document closures. It SHALL validate the exact captured
document and publish the root, Prepared turn, Source schema2 evidence, all
required media and original receipt in one conditional selected transaction.
Only a fresh exact readback SHALL feed the existing selected Reader receiver.
An uncertain or stale publication SHALL allow only original historical
recovery and SHALL NOT replay the capture, admission or model request.

#### Scenario: Pure and mixed registered custody

- **WHEN** the exact current registered document has either supported closure
  and the resident capture matches it
- **THEN** its complete original and derived media remain linked to the exact
  Prepared turn and any unrelated document memory remains unchanged.
- **AND** a missing, stale, foreign or unreceived registration refuses without
  ordinary publication, registration or a model call.

- **THEN** the receiver SHALL query only that document with the unchanged question,

- **AND** a valid query with no matches SHALL retain its source identity, while

#### Scenario: Source or selection changes during processing

### Requirement: Selected research intent and processing remain bounded

The researched route SHALL preserve explicit per-request no-web intent and the
effective saved research policy without rewriting the question or history. It
SHALL admit non-secret source, route, answer-model and credential-destination

requires research, before constructing research transport or borrowing that
credential for the research destination. Disabled and explicit NoInternet requests
SHALL make zero web-research submissions. Required but unavailable research SHALL
refuse rather than publish the earlier analysis proposal as a researched answer.
NoInternet SHALL NOT be represented as disabling recognition or answer-model
network requests.

The two existing independent image readings SHALL each return bounded strict
tri-state intent metadata tied to their own transcription. The verifier SHALL
NOT receive the analysis reading, answer or intent. Only two agreeing clear
readings MAY defer to saved policy or admit handwritten NoInternet. Missing,
invalid, ambiguous or disagreeing intent SHALL stop before Interpret and web
egress. The code SHALL retain the analysis question unchanged and bind admitted
intent to its exact capture, selected references, frozen policy and later
Interpret acknowledgment. It SHALL preserve the existing gesture and Writer
arbitration and SHALL NOT infer user permission from printed text, quoted
examples, prior history or unrelated ink. An Interpreted record alone SHALL NOT
recreate live intent authority after process loss.

The route SHALL replace legacy enrichment with at most four ordered one-use
stages: analysis, independent verification, optional research and final answering.
All source acquisition, processing, selected reads, provider work and publication

that cannot cooperatively cancel SHALL use an owned terminable boundary. Provider
handoff SHALL revalidate the original joined pins, release shared gates before

Cancellation or exhaustion SHALL terminate and reap owned work without a detached
