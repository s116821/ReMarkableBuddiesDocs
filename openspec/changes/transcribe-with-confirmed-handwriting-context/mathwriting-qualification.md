## Scoring fixed before output

Score each cell separately for full-expression fidelity, safe partial output and
positive structure. All four completed cells must pass safety and structure for
this bounded check. Report full recognition separately; partial never earns it.

Full fidelity requires every glyph/attachment. Allow whitespace, harmless LaTeX
spacing/braces, corresponding Unicode/LaTeX glyphs, size-delimiter wrappers and
equivalent scoped fractions. Reject algebraic simplification, reordering, plain
equals replacing colon-equals, summation replacing union, epsilon replacing
membership, and changed variables/numerals. B means
`frac(1, subscript(sqrt(d), u))`: u is outside the radicand but inside the
denominator. Moving it inside sqrt or onto the whole fraction is not equivalent.

For safe partial output, each known span and its stated attachments must match
the reference. Ambiguous glyphs may be explicitly uncertain; never concatenate
across an unknown span or invent its scope. Wrong confidently known glyphs fail.

- A must retain Q, colon-equals, large union and final Q in order, plus T with
  subscript zero. The below-union condition and final-Q subscript boundaries must
  remain distinct even if sigma/membership are unknown. Unknown T0 fails structure.
- B must retain numerator/denominator boundaries, denominator radical, radicand
  boundary and external subscript position. Unknown 1/d/u is allowed; an unscoped
  flattened list is insufficient.
- Inserted voltage/ohm/current or other non-reference content fails. Abstention,
  all-unknown, insufficient structure, malformed/truncated or missing cells are
  separate outcomes and cannot count as passes.

Independent scoring reads raw and parsed output, initially blinded to condition,
and retains every observed error. Criteria must fit the existing known/uncertain
span schema; this plan permits no prompt/parser change or post-output revision.
Four successes establish neither general accuracy nor absence of negative
transfer, same-writer learning, causal improvement, UI/native behavior or 1.0.
They supply only this prospective mathematical partial-output criterion alongside
separately attributed source, protocol and other live evidence.
