# Declared research policy and evidence composition

## Why

REM-4 requires external verification/enrichment by default, explicit no-internet
behavior and useful provenance. Existing frozen source/memory data does not yet
represent a research decision or bounded research evidence. The router must retain
the original request/history; research cannot silently become an optional local
success when a provider is absent or unavailable.

## What changes

Add transient research-policy/evidence composition after the existing frozen
source/optional-memory context. Default-required research, per-request no-internet
and a separately labeled configured-disabled alternative have explicit precedence.
A replaceable synchronous Provider receives the complete immutable context and
returns bounded untrusted evidence bound to that exact historical observation.
Keep complete-empty, nonempty-complete, partial and unavailable outcomes distinct.

## Scope

Implement and verify the ordinary data seam using authored synthetic Providers,
real local Store/Index/Memory and existing context validators. No live search or
model provider, external network request, installed Reader consumption, prompt,
tablet/RE/service action, configuration persistence/Manager change, registry,
credential, dependency/SDK/CI graph or durable evidence schema is selected.
Synthetic results cannot establish delivered live research or runtime acceptance.
