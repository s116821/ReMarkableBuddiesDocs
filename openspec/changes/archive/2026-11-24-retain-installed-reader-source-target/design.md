## Publication

An accepted installed Reader interpretation publishes its user turn and a new revision of the linked resident capture in one Store transaction. The target refers to the complete provider overview in the exact saved capture. Document/page identity, original media, viewport dimensions and crop provenance come from existing capture facts. The assistant turn contains the exact answer and references that same capture.

The normal continuation consumer keeps its eligibility rules and attaches the same historical images. When the target is present it includes the origin turn, captured document and page, and the coordinates on the overview association. Older captures retain their legacy representation and omit a target. No role text or previously rendered QA block is parsed for a coordinate or rewritten.

The resident capture payload uses revision 2 when a target is present. It retains the existing Source envelope, capture ID, source correspondence, image descriptors and media. Readers that understand only revision 1 refuse the revised record. Revision 1 records without a target require no migration.
