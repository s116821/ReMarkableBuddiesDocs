## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared whole-archive custody inspection
Both companions SHALL offer explicit bounded read-only inspection of a compatible archive already on a verified wired tablet, using the fixed existing producer and closed reduced report. Exact protected path input SHALL clear from signal and actual DOM at every defined lifetime boundary. Format/media/settings presence SHALL describe validated custody without source freshness, provider, restore or native authority.

#### Scenario: Complete or intentionally incomplete archive
- **WHEN** the fixed inspector returns a valid supported whole-archive report
- **THEN** both companions display truthful completeness and settings-presence consequences with optional exact counts; missing promised bytes or malformed reports never become healthy empty data.

#### Scenario: Pending inspection is invalidated
- **WHEN** Stop, refresh, peer admission, disconnect, identity change or page lifetime invalidates an inspection
- **THEN** protected draft/DOM clears and a late result cannot repopulate current state or authorize another action.

### Requirement: Policy-only preview and once-only export
The consumer SHALL separately preview only known consequences and require explicit confirmation to export selected historical custody to a new on-tablet destination. It SHALL NOT invent producer Prepare authority, frozen source counts, conditional intent, guaranteed success or portable settings. Possible dispatch SHALL consume original authority once while preserving exact private original destination/input and truthful uncertainty.

#### Scenario: Preview before export
- **WHEN** a user prepares a new export destination
- **THEN** the UI explains content/exclusions/current-data preservation without producer dispatch or invented counts, and only a separate admitted confirmation may invoke export once.

#### Scenario: Output is lost after possible export
- **WHEN** export may have dispatched but no complete valid reply establishes its result
- **THEN** original history remains unknown with no automatic retry, overwrite, staging cleanup or second export under that reference.

#### Scenario: New attempt or bounded capacity
- **WHEN** a genuinely new deliberate preview has room, or all eight original slots are occupied
- **THEN** the former preserves every older packet/history/deadline and the latter refuses without dispatch, renewal or eviction.

### Requirement: Original-only checks and guarded bounded integration
Read-only checks SHALL use only the original host-custodied destination, preserve original export history independently from later observed custody, and enforce finite expiry/invocation/cancellation bounds. Both real host surfaces SHALL integrate the fourteenth maintenance owner, reciprocal peer invalidation and observation quiescence while preserving all156old directed relationships and existing identity/privacy/framing limits.

#### Scenario: Archive is observed after uncertain export
- **WHEN** the original destination later contains a valid archive
- **THEN** current readable custody is shown while the earlier export remains unknown; no publisher identity or original receipt is fabricated.

#### Scenario: Check refuses or original expires
- **WHEN** a read-only check is unsupported/unavailable/corrupt or private original custody expires
- **THEN** uncertainty and safe bounded historical metadata remain without replay, rollback inference, reconstruction or lifetime renewal.

#### Scenario: Shared I/O is occupied
- **WHEN** another maintenance owner holds I/O
- **THEN** Busy precedes hooks/cancellation/producer work, preserves the current owner and cannot dispatch through an observation race.

#### Scenario: Late cancellation targets an older action
- **WHEN** a stale captured invocation is canceled after a successor owns I/O
- **THEN** only its exact identity/reference/invocation may be fenced and the successor remains untouched.
