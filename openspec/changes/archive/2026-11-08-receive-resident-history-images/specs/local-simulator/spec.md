## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Maintained resident receiving evidence uses production receivers
The simulator SHALL support a declared synthetic resident-history case through
the existing Reader/Writer/notebook receiving and bounded qualification runner.
It SHALL check exact derivative identities, ordered associations, reducer digest,
actual returned history and retained drafts. Modeled correspondence SHALL NOT be
presented as native currentness, output completion or live-model qualification.

#### Scenario: Offline resident conversation sequence
- **WHEN** the maintained resident case runs in scripted mode
- **THEN** the real receivers preserve its four intended provider images and the original history through the seven-stage sequence without credential resolution.

#### Scenario: Live evidence remains separate
- **WHEN** a live resident case is prepared but not independently selected and executed
- **THEN** it grants no provider dispatch and prior ImageUse evidence is not relabeled as resident acceptance.
