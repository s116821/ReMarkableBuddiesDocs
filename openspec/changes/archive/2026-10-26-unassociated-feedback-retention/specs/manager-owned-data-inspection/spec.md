## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Strict versioned feedback accounting consumer
Manager SHALL retain the existing exact report1 five-domain validator and support report2 through a separate exact shape/version/domain branch, including bounded generation-format evidence. Unknown versions/fields/domains SHALL refuse. The new historical category SHALL be shown only when supported, with missing/uninspected distinct from zero, no raw observation content, no inherited cleanup action and no display/live-state inference. Existing boot/connection/request-epoch and nonmutating browser/Electron boundaries SHALL remain. Source: current strict report1 parser and coordinated independent namespace support.

#### Scenario: Old and new producer
- **WHEN** a valid legacy report1 or supported feature report2 arrives from the same current observed target
- **THEN** only the corresponding supported categories/accounting are displayed, without widening report1 or hiding new-domain bytes in an unrelated category.

#### Scenario: Unknown, malformed or stale response
- **WHEN** a version/domain/field is unsupported, evidence is partial or the target/request changed
- **THEN** Manager shows unavailable/unsupported or explicit partial state, discards stale results, and grants no cleanup, rollback, runtime or native action.

### Requirement: Coordinated consumer-first adoption
The report2 consumer SHALL be delivered before compatible producer feature adoption. Publication/version matching SHALL NOT automatically prepare storage or enable retention callbacks. Existing cleanup-owned files SHALL be reconciled through explicit handback and all three affected repositories SHALL complete one coordinated specification/implementation lifecycle. Source: independent component compatibility and scoped ownership policy.

#### Scenario: Consumer not yet ready
- **WHEN** a producer/consumer or management path lacks qualified feature3 support
- **THEN** adoption/production wiring remains gated and unsupported behavior is explicit; neither validator weakening nor a claimed safe downgrade substitutes for qualification.
