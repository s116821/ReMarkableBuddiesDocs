# Bounded ordinary conversation-root candidates

## Why
Selective local cleanup needs an explicitly selected conversation UUID. The
delivered inspect/prepare/apply/status producer and Ledger inspection require that
UUID; aggregate owned-data accounting exposes no conversation-ID collection.
Provide a deliberately narrow discovery step without interpreting a candidate as
eligible history, deletion permission, or a current native-page association.

## What changes
Add a separate parameterless Linux `--inspect-conversations` route and closed
version-one candidate report. Decode persisted ordinary Conversation records under
existing-only, inactive-owner Store/mode custody and one cumulative work budget.
Return at most sixteen sorted current value-root IDs as a complete bounded result;
never truncate, expose content, or add fields/values to existing closed wires.

## Scope and delivery
One Buddy/central Docs pair, independent of the completed deletion delivery.
Candidate discovery does not evaluate descendant settlement or authorize mutation.
No Manager consumer, native lookup, source-image browsing, provider, credentials,
repair, staging cleanup, physical purge, service mutation or tablet operation.
Complete concrete precode review before source edits, then implementation,
separately attributed execution evidence, canonical sync/archive and final paired
review in this single coordinated delivery. No planning-only merge.
