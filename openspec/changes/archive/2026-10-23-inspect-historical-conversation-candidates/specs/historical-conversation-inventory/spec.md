## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete bounded ordinary root candidates
The Linux producer SHALL expose a separate parameterless conversation-root candidate
inventory with at most sixteen unique sorted current ordinary schema-one value-root
IDs. It SHALL classify complete retained Conversation history with original domain
decoders, exclude singleton tombstoned roots, refuse conflicting/unsupported/type-
reused roots and too many candidates without a partial list, and distinguish a
complete empty candidate set from unavailable state. Candidate presence SHALL NOT
evaluate descendant eligibility or replace explicit selection and fresh existing
conversation inspect/prepare. Source: selective owned-data management and the
delivered explicit-ID conversation maintenance contract; contract.md.

#### Scenario: Mixed retained and current roots
- **WHEN** valid ordinary retained history contains live and tombstoned roots within the admitted budgets
- **THEN** the complete list contains only singleton current value-root IDs in deterministic order, with no text, native IDs, eligibility or deletion permission.

#### Scenario: Ambiguous or oversized discovery
- **WHEN** root identity/history/heads are unsupported or conflicting, discovery work cannot fit, or more than sixteen current candidates exist
- **THEN** the producer emits an explicit sanitized refusal with no partial candidate list or fabricated complete zero result and changes nothing.

### Requirement: Existing-only bounded payload-read custody
The inventory SHALL reuse safe existing ordinary Store/mode and fixed inactive-unit
observation under shared whole-generation and cumulative read/work bounds, retaining
custody through output/failure. It SHALL decode owned persisted records without
claiming metadata-only behavior, reserve work before decoding/visits, preserve
staging/history/media/original documents and never initialize, repair, mutate or
contact credentials/providers/native pages. Non-Linux SHALL refuse before input,
Config, storage or OS access. Source: existing maintenance custody and budget rules;
contract.md.

#### Scenario: Owner or custody unavailable
- **WHEN** inactivity, original headers/Config/lease, supported ordinary mode or bounded recovery cannot be established
- **THEN** no candidate state or mutation authority is emitted and every existing file remains unchanged.

### Requirement: Independent closed discovery report
The inventory SHALL use a distinct closed version-one report and early route,
preserving all existing operation values, reports and defaults. Complete/refusal
and output-loss behavior SHALL be bounded and explicit; reports SHALL grant no
native/runtime, physical-reclamation or mutation authority. Source: independent
producer compatibility and public/manual requirements; contract.md.

#### Scenario: Early refusal and lost output
- **WHEN** invocation is unsupported/conflicting or output fails after admission
- **THEN** ordinary runtime startup is avoided, protected values are not echoed in supported reports, custody is retained through output failure and no second report or data action follows.
