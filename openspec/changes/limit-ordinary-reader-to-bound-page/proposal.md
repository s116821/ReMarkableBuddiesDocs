## Why

Ordinary Reader starts with the page immediately left of Buddy, the current conversation, and relevant memory. Opening a book or retaining a notebook transcription currently can lead to document-wide work that exceeds the scope and latency of an ordinary question.

## What changes

- Keep source-page submission and continuation scoped to the bound original page.
- Keep current question ink or typed text separate from answer-source material. An unbound blank conversation has no original page.
- Keep automatic lookup off the ordinary path when other-page material requires acquisition, extraction, OCR, revalidation or index construction. A ready, current, relevant text lookup may be considered as a future optional fast path.
- Preserve the original-file library and explicitly selected Writer and researched Reader workflows. A user interaction for broad Reader scope will be specified separately.

## Impact

The installed Reader source-gesture path and the shared typed Reader receiving boundary. Existing configurations remain readable. This change does not change research settings or initiate device activity.
