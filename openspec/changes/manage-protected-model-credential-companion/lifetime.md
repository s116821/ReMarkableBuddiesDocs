Ready client expiry starts at Begin invocation, not a later old-ready render, so delayed observation cannot extend entry. Host checks its own authoritative monotonic ready deadline. Confidential packet deadline uses host monotonic time fixed at first possible apply; checks are atomic with token/epoch ownership before every I/O. Expiry clears owned packet/buffer references and makes status unavailable without replacing intent, changing last retained unresolved state or releasing someone else's token. Timers are backed by checks at every entry/return; unrelated activity cannot renew an expired record.

Encoded retained confidential storage per original is bounded by three16384-byte components (original prepared bytes, status input and validated intent representation), at most8 originals, plus one ready binding and one bounded active operation. These are encoded-size bounds, not a byte-exact JS heap/zeroization guarantee. Child processes are supervised and private parser exceptions are not retained. Immutable JavaScript/Python strings, IPC/SSH and OS copies cannot be physically erased by promise; only bounded ownership/references/mutable-buffer clearing are claimed.

A status successful desired observation is current-file reconciliation only. Removing its confidential packet or active pending row must not label historical publication/authentication verified. Refusal/timeout/cancel/packet expiry never resolves uncertainty. An unavailable private original has no fallback reconstruction from current reference, present bit, new secret or caller-supplied hashes. The30-minute deadline and8-tombstone policy are explicit design choices requiring independent precode acceptance; they are not properties of the delivered producer or other seven lanes.

## Exact expiry transitions and user presentation

The host fixes `original_deadline = monotonic_now + 1800000ms` atomically when it seals the original and marks the first possible apply dispatch, immediately before invoking apply transport. A save that expires its ready/aggregate budget before this point is not-dispatched and creates no uncertain original. Apply gets only the remaining aggregate budget; the original deadline never extends that budget. After possible dispatch, cancellation or an incomplete/late result leaves uncertainty. A genuinely complete, validated original-bound result may settle only while its token and relevant deadlines remain valid.

Before status starts, require a live original packet and positive remaining original lifetime. Its child timeout is the minimum of 12000ms, the remaining status operation budget and remaining original lifetime. If expiry occurs while status is active, invalidate that status generation, terminate its owned child, purge private material, return unknown with null current/report and retain the safe last unknown/unresolved marker with status_available:false. Late output cannot settle or refill that record. Safe identities and the last retained unknown/unresolved state survive; no secret, hash, binding, intent or previous presence assertion survives as reconciliation authority.

The pending row says: “Outcome unknown. Original check is no longer available; the current saved file cannot prove what this operation did.” Its Check action is disabled. When capacity is exhausted, say: “Eight unresolved operations are retained. Another saved-file change is unavailable in this host session.” Do not offer a retry or automatic restart. Retained/status/cancel are still safe local operations; cancel cannot erase the row or free a slot. Fresh Begin/Save are refused at capacity. Neither a reference/config edit nor a new inspection reconstructs the old original or frees capacity. Separate user-authorized future work may inspect current state, without claiming historical settlement.

A host-process restart discards its in-memory inventory and private packets; there is deliberately no vault or recovery store. A surviving renderer must retain its own safe unknown warning and must not interpret the new empty host inventory as resolution. A full page/process restart may also lose that renderer warning, so this proposal makes no durable cross-restart uncertainty recovery claim. Restart is not presented as a recovery or capacity-clearing workflow. These limits require explicit independent review before implementation.
