## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Product source-target preparation
Normal supported target-producing startup SHALL prepare an eligible Store before ready-only admission and runtime owners. Selected first-absence admission SHALL carry its existing qualified GET-only witness and original allowance through local promotion/rechecks. No manual scratch prerequisite, empty-selection inference or hidden repeated fetch SHALL substitute.

#### Scenario: Selected policy is absent
- **WHEN** startup has no locally selected preference
- **THEN** existing qualified absence custody is preserved exactly or startup refuses without target/native/model authority.
