## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Separate notebook-inclusive inspection in both companions
Manager SHALL consume report3through a disjoint closed request/parser branch in
the browser and Electron hosts, preserving report1/2and actual connection/boot/
request-epoch, Busy, cancellation and privacy paths. It SHALL invoke only the fixed
v3CLI on a verified wired target; unsupported producers and partial/uninspected
categories SHALL remain explicit without fallback scraping or invented zeroes.
Source: current shared owned-data consumer and the proposed versioned producer.

#### Scenario: Notebook metadata with large exact byte counts
- **WHEN** the same valid report3is returned through either supported host factory
- **THEN** the shared UI preserves exact observed counts and unknowns, labels notebook/reference subtotals nonadditive and grants no cleanup/restore/native authority.

#### Scenario: Unsupported, malformed or stale successor result
- **WHEN** the CLI is unsupported or output is malformed, partial, late, canceled or belongs to another verified session
- **THEN** no stale/rounded/secret-bearing report becomes current, no ordinary Reader starts and released report1/2workflows retain their qualified behavior.

### Requirement: Coordinated successor producer and consumer qualification
The producer, central contract and both Manager hosts SHALL be delivered as one
reviewed coordinated change with exact version/compatibility evidence. Source
presence or a synthetic parser case SHALL NOT establish actual host/native or
backup/restore readiness. Source: independent-release delivery contract.

#### Scenario: Consumer qualification remains incomplete
- **WHEN** either actual Manager host lacks qualified successor handling
- **THEN** that support remains explicit unfinished scope and the central feature is not archived as a completed planning-only delivery.

### Requirement: Strict report3 raw admission and exact relationships
The separate consumer SHALL admit raw report3 only after fatal UTF8/BOM, one-frame/LF, duplicate/closed-key, canonical numeric-token, decimal-string/u64 and frozen semantic relationship validation. Raw output SHALL be at most32768 bytes including LF; count/format spellings SHALL NOT be normalized into acceptance. Storage/shared/subset sums, checked overflow/null issues, complete map coverage and zero-reference requirements SHALL match the frozen contract. Report1/2 parser/types/framing SHALL remain unchanged. Source: qualified producer2d7d8e9, frozen contract.md/schema.json/vectors.json and consumer-design.md.

#### Scenario: Numeric or relational forgery
- **WHEN** output uses a fractional/exponent version or format, malformed decimal count, inconsistent physical/notebook totals, forged complete state or invalid overflow claim
- **THEN** neither host displays a healthy report, even if ordinary JSON parsing or structural schema validation would succeed.

#### Scenario: Unknown and uninspected data
- **WHEN** a valid partial report has an unknown byte aggregate or omits an uninspected category
- **THEN** both consumers retain Unknown or Not checked distinctly from observed zero and never synthesize a complete or healthy-empty result.

### Requirement: Closed private reduction and nonadditive presentation
Both hosts SHALL validate a single identity-only request of at most256 bytes and expose the separately validated closed21-key public view defined in consumer-design.md. Raw owner/current-generation identities, custody/basis/exclusion details, raw bytes, diagnostic text, paths and protected content SHALL remain private. Public results SHALL be at most16384 bytes and preserve exact decimal strings, observed current/retained formats and nonadditive flags. Notebook metadata SHALL be labeled included in existing totals, not added again or interpreted as requests, visible turns, liveness or feature activation. Source: fixed producer report3 scope and consumer-design.md.

#### Scenario: Valid private metadata in a producer report
- **WHEN** an admitted report contains owner/current-generation identifiers and fixed custody metadata
- **THEN** the host validates them before reducing to display accounting, and neither bridge nor renderer receives the private fields or arbitrary raw report.

#### Scenario: Shared references and included notebook records
- **WHEN** references are shared or notebook metadata overlaps Source/Conversation and physical accounting
- **THEN** the UI preserves the exact observed values while clearly marking included/nonadditive subtotals without inventing reclaimable, allocated or additive space totals.

### Requirement: Bounded report3 invocation and shared ownership
The new capability SHALL use only fixed no-input --inspect-owned-data-v3 through verified Linux/Windows wired callbacks, with256-byte private input,49152-byte raw child envelope,32768-byte stdout,8192-byte discarded stderr and remaining2s/8s/12s/13s/14s channel/collection/child/host/UI budgets. It SHALL acquire shared maintenance ownership before invalidation/I/O, preserve original request epochs and session generations, and release only its invocation. Stop/hidden/pagehide SHALL clear local delivery without a delayed global cancellation of newer work; external observation/disconnect cancellation SHALL revoke the read through existing ownership hooks. No retry, scraping, service stop or ordinary Reader fallback is permitted. Source: current Manager7d8faaa host seams and consumer-design.md.

#### Scenario: Busy or late read beside a Local-only unknown operation
- **WHEN** another maintenance owner is active, or an old report3 read finishes after Stop, refresh, cancellation or connection/boot change
- **THEN** Busy performs no competing I/O/invalidation, late output cannot restore a view or release another owner, and retained Local-only uncertainty remains unchanged.

#### Scenario: Bound or unsupported command
- **WHEN** the fixed command is unsupported or exceeds framing, size or remaining deadline bounds
- **THEN** both hosts preserve unsupported/unavailable status without fallback data or a second command and the older explicit report1/2 workflow remains available manually.
