## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deliberate existing-Store Local-only choice
Both Manager companions SHALL offer a separate Local-only choice capability using fresh admitted mode inspection and explicit confirmation against an existing valid owned Store. The fixed writer SHALL receive only the privately held original conditional token, host-generated nonnil operation UUID and Local-only choice. Existing identity-only saved-mode inspection SHALL remain read-only and unchanged. This capability SHALL NOT initialize/repair a Store, edit configuration/bindings, choose cloud modes, stop services, convert data or activate runtime/provider/native work.

#### Scenario: Review then choose
- **WHEN** a supported existing Store is freshly inspected and the user confirms Local only before the preview expires
- **THEN** the host submits that original conditional choice once and both companions distinguish saved mode metadata from unverified runtime readiness.

#### Scenario: Missing, Busy or stale Store
- **WHEN** inspection or choice is unavailable, Busy, unsupported or conflicts with the original precondition
- **THEN** the UI preserves the truthful refusal or uncertainty without initialization, service stopping, implicit rebase or an invented Local-only result.

### Requirement: Private bounded original authority
The host SHALL retain the immutable original conditional packet and validate strict one-line report-v1 input/output of at most16KiB with activation_performed=false. Successful receipts SHALL match original operation, expected digest, request fingerprint and choice; current/historical classification SHALL agree with actual epoch and mode. Both bridges SHALL expose separately validated reduced public state without raw tokens, actors, operation UUIDs, fingerprints, binding/root details or receipts. Ready previews SHALL expire after120 seconds; possible-dispatch records SHALL have a capacity of8 per host lifetime without evicting unresolved records.

#### Scenario: Same mode under a new epoch
- **WHEN** a new intentional Local-only choice records a different epoch or an old receipt is returned with a later same-kind snapshot
- **THEN** the consumer treats the new choice as a mutation and the old receipt as historical rather than synthesizing no-change/current success.

#### Scenario: Invalid or private response
- **WHEN** a response is malformed, duplicated, partial, oversized, unsupported in version or inconsistent with the original intent
- **THEN** no healthy success is displayed and no private authority leaks through either bridge or renderer state.

### Requirement: One dispatch and retained original uncertainty
Each ready reference SHALL permit at most one deliberate writer dispatch. Cancellation before dispatch SHALL invalidate it; cancellation, timeout, disconnect, failed/incomplete or untrusted output after possible dispatch SHALL preserve an unknown original outcome unless a validated original receipt or proven complete pre-mutation refusal establishes otherwise. Current saved metadata SHALL NOT resolve an unknown original. No action SHALL automatically retry choose, replace its token, silently discard uncertainty or create another choice for that target while its unknown record remains in the host lifetime.

#### Scenario: Commit followed by failed output
- **WHEN** a choice may have committed but its response fails, is incomplete or is lost
- **THEN** both companions retain original uncertainty and do not infer no change, retry, or mark a later matching Local-only observation as original success.

#### Scenario: Duplicate confirmation
- **WHEN** confirmation repeats or queues after the original reference was consumed
- **THEN** no second writer invocation occurs and the original record remains authoritative.

### Requirement: Separate current observation
A deliberate current-state refresh SHALL execute only fixed inspect, preserving original operation history and uncertainty. It SHALL NOT obtain a receipt by replaying choose, claim original reconciliation, or imply current service/sync/provider health. Retained history SHALL survive browser reload and session invalidation within the host lifetime; host process restart SHALL NOT be presented as reconciliation.

#### Scenario: Newer saved state after unknown
- **WHEN** the user refreshes current mode after an uncertain original choice and receives a newer snapshot
- **THEN** the UI labels that current observation separately while retaining the original unknown outcome and disabling another choice for that target.

### Requirement: Shared ownership and stale-result rejection
Both companions SHALL use the shared maintenance owner and fixed identity-verified Linux/Windows wired adapters, existing bounded operation deadlines, host-issued response epochs and renderer generations. Competing maintenance, same-ID tablet refresh, cancel/disconnect, hidden/pagehide and disposal SHALL revoke ready/pending authority; late or foreign responses SHALL NOT restore it. Busy/retained operations SHALL NOT cancel or release another invocation's owner.

#### Scenario: Delayed result after invalidation
- **WHEN** a ready or dispatched operation is invalidated and its delayed result later arrives, including after an unchanged-ID refresh
- **THEN** it cannot repopulate the new session or overwrite retained uncertainty, and unrelated maintenance ownership stays intact.
