## Why

An older indeterminate native insertion can leave a later Reader question on the same notebook unable to place its answer. A reboot ends the original runtime. The current service can verify the exact original page and order before permitting independent placement.

## What Changes

- Allow a separate ordinary Reader conversation to pass the historical placement check when fresh later-boot native facts show the exact original page and order and the old target is absent.
- Preserve the historical attempt and normal insertion guard. Retain the existing refusal for ambiguous placement and effects on the original conversation.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `reader-answer-pages` specifies exact evidence for later-boot independent Reader placement.

## Impact

The resident Reader placement check and its regression change. Existing requests, storage format, rendering and model behavior remain in their current scope.
