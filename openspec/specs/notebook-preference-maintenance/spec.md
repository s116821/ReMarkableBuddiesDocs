# Notebook preference maintenance

## Purpose
Permit only the existing product and research conditional writers on ready ordinary notebook storage. See the [admission contract](../../changes/archive/2026-11-06-save-notebook-product-research-settings/contract.md). Other settings and runtime/native authority remain separate.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Scoped existing notebook preference admission
Product and research conditional setting publication SHALL admit a ready existing ordinary local notebook Store through a distinct narrowly scoped admission path under the original exclusive custody. It SHALL validate original owned identity/current generation, notebook readiness/contract/capabilities, complete recovery, local manifests, absent/empty selected/history state and validated absent/LocalOnly mode without initialization/adoption/repair. Existing legacy admission and all other constructors/callers SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Ready ordinary notebook
- **WHEN** a supported notebook Store and current original preference intent satisfy all custody/readiness/ordinary-state conditions
- **THEN** the existing product or research publisher may change only its original exact configuration target while preserving every notebook/data/identity/current/metadata/media byte.

#### Scenario: Unsupported or busy notebook state
- **WHEN** ownership/readiness/layout/history/mode/current input is busy, unavailable, corrupt, interrupted or outside the supported ordinary state
- **THEN** publication refuses in existing categories without fallback, data/config change, initialization, stage cleanup or source repair.

#### Scenario: Another configuration or maintenance caller
- **WHEN** the original generic config opener or excluded setting/credential/mode/outbox path encounters notebook storage
- **THEN** its existing notebook refusal is preserved rather than inheriting the new scoped preference admission.

### Requirement: Existing conditional publication and uncertainty preserved
The two writers SHALL retain current raw-declaration target semantics, exact original input/path/base/target conditions, mode/report custody through consuming output, publication fault outcomes and disk-readback/restart-required distinction. Original Status SHALL remain read-only and SHALL NOT repeat Apply or infer running/native activation.

#### Scenario: Successful config-only publication
- **WHEN** the original target is published and exactly read back under notebook custody
- **THEN** only the requested product/research declarations change and the existing verified report (product restart-required; research runtime unverified) is returned without service/runtime activation.

#### Scenario: Ambiguous publication or output failure
- **WHEN** publication/readback may have happened or output immediately fails or accepts a prefix before failure
- **THEN** the original uncertain outcome and ownership/error rules remain, no automatic retry/replacement report occurs and later Status only observes the original desired file.
