## Requirements

### Requirement: Explicit shared bounded conversation discovery
Both Manager companions SHALL expose the optional identity-only explicit read over the fixed unchanged Linux conversation inventory, validate its complete closed report and show only sorted Buddy-owned root IDs/count with snapshot limits under contract.md. Empty complete and all busy/unavailable/conflict/unsupported/too-large refusals SHALL remain distinct. Source: canonical historical-conversation-inventory and its delivered exact contract/manual.

#### Scenario: Complete candidates or complete empty snapshot
- **WHEN** a matching observed wired user explicitly requests supported discovery
- **THEN** only the fixed no-input producer runs and its complete0..16UUID result or inspected-empty state is displayed without text/title/native IDs, eligibility, mutation, runtime or physical-reclamation authority.

#### Scenario: Refused or malformed discovery
- **WHEN** bounds, framing, strict shape, flags, IDs/count/order, version, custody or identity fails or a producer refusal arrives
- **THEN** the corresponding fixed bounded refusal replaces current candidates without truncation, false zero, cached fallback, alternate command, source repair, service stop or implicit selection/deletion.

### Requirement: Eleven-owner and stale-read isolation
The consumer SHALL compose the real eleventh maintenance owner in both existing factories, preserve all ten earlier operations/original uncertainties, and enforce identity/quiescence/reciprocal clearing and bounded local Stop under contract.md. Source: current ten-owner MaintenanceIO, delivered read/cleanup lifetimes and actual queued-observation quiescence.

#### Scenario: Busy and reciprocal admitted action
- **WHEN** a peer already owns maintenance or discovery/a peer begins admitted work
- **THEN** known Busy causes no I/O/erasure/hook/cancel side effect, admitted work clears idle private grants/actual sensitive DOM and stale snapshots before relevant awaits, and unresolved originals/expiry remain independent without replay or renewal.

#### Scenario: Stopped queued read or later target/owner
- **WHEN** Stop, deadline, observation/target change or peer action supersedes a pending read or quiescence continuation
- **THEN** no stale result or queued continuation refills/dispatches and no old timer/finally cancels or releases a newer owner; Stop detaches locally without remote cancellation or token release.

### Requirement: Guarded narrow consumer and attributed paired delivery
The new consumer SHALL preserve exact HTTP/main-frame IPC/stream/privacy admission, public manual equivalents and independent current Windows/Linux source/retained qualification within its own complete paired lifecycle. Source: protected Manager host surfaces and central contribution/OpenSpec workflow.

#### Scenario: Untrusted caller or implicit action
- **WHEN** an invocation has wrong origin/frame/URL/arity/body/extra selector or no deliberate user request
- **THEN** the fixed capability refuses with sanitized labels and no raw transport, extra API, automatic polling/persistence/selection or write authority.

#### Scenario: Qualified delivery and source provenance
- **WHEN** the consumer is proposed for delivery
- **THEN** unchanged producer and earlier failure provenance remain distinct from110/old90factory,29incoming/10outgoing and both-OS guarded source/retained evidence; independent review precedes own canonical/manual sync/archive and normal current-main paired delivery, with publication and broad MVP separately observed.
