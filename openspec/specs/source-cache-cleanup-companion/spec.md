# Source-cache cleanup companion

## Purpose

Provide deliberate bounded one-slot derived source-cache cleanup in both Manager companions. The exact [consumer wire and lifecycle contract](../../changes/archive/2026-10-26-manage-source-cache-cleanup-companion/contract.md) defines complete report admission, private original custody, one possible apply, invocation-bound cancellation and honest current-state reconciliation. Inventory remains metadata-only and grants no cleanup authority. See the [public companion guide](../../../docs/manager-source-cache-cleanup.md) for manual routing and qualification limits.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Shared deliberate fresh one-slot cleanup preview
Both companions SHALL offer deliberate selected-ID preparation and separate one-shot confirmation through the fixed delivered producer under contract.md, with complete report admission before bounded safe reduction. Selection and metadata inventory SHALL grant no mutation/validity/freshness authority and source history/original documents SHALL remain outside the operation. Source basis: REM42 selective cleanup, canonical prepared-source-cache-cleanup and source-cache-inventory-companion.

#### Scenario: User selects and prepares a derived slot
- **WHEN** an observed wired user explicitly chooses a canonical candidate or enters its ID and requests fresh preparation
- **THEN** only a validated present selected derived slot yields an unexpired host-owned preview/reference, with size/consequences but no intent/hash/path exposure, automatic confirmation or inventory-row authority.

#### Scenario: Unsafe, absent or incompatible preparation
- **WHEN** selection/report/schema/identity/ownership/custody/bounds admission fails or the producer observes absence/refusal
- **THEN** fixed bounded states grant no preview/cleanup permission, no partial/zero success, repair, arbitrary path, alternate command or installed-version inference.

### Requirement: Private original one-apply custody and honest reconciliation
The host SHALL bind exact prepared original packets to verified connection/boot and finite lifetimes, permit at most one possible apply, preserve unknown originals without renewal/retry/rollback, and expose original-only read-only current-state reconciliation under contract.md. Source basis: unchanged prepared cleanup intent/report and existing original-bound consumer custody.

#### Scenario: Confirmation or uncertain output
- **WHEN** a valid ready reference is deliberately confirmed once and exact readback completes or output is missing/changed/timed out after possible dispatch
- **THEN** the original is consumed once and the result is respectively applied or retained unknown, never silently refreshed/reprepared/retried or used to delete a replacement.

#### Scenario: Original status, cancellation or expiry
- **WHEN** an original is explicitly checked, stopped, superseded or expires
- **THEN** unchanged/conflict/refusal never acknowledges deletion, absence says only currently absent with historical deleter unverified, private expiry is not renewed and old cancellation/result/deadline cannot affect another owner/editor.

### Requirement: Ten-owner guarded shared delivery
The consumer SHALL preserve existing nine operations/private lifetimes and compose real shared ownership, explicit UI invalidation, observation quiescence and independent Windows/Linux source/retained qualification through its complete own paired lifecycle. Source basis: current MaintenanceIO/factories, local observation-pause correction and central contribution workflow.

#### Scenario: Busy, queued observation or late callback
- **WHEN** another owner is Busy, a poll is queued before host dispatch, or Stop/14s expiry supersedes an awaited result
- **THEN** Busy performs no host hook/cancel/I/O, queued observation must quiesce successfully before dispatch without global cancel, private/view clearing is synchronous where admitted and no stale continuation dispatches or refills a newer view.

#### Scenario: Guarded explicit consumer delivery
- **WHEN** untrusted input or the final consumer candidate enters HTTP/main-frame IPC and delivery review
- **THEN** strict caps/identity/privacy and90 directed ownership/25 peer-boundary controls preserve the old72 cases, original producer/failure attribution and unrelated native files, followed by own canonical/manual sync/archive and normal final paired review/delivery with publication observed separately.
