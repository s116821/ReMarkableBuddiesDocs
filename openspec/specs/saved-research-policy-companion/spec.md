# Saved research-policy companion

## Purpose
Provide deliberate saved-only research-policy editing in both Manager companions. The complete
[consumer contract](../../changes/archive/2026-10-25-edit-saved-research-policy-companion/contract.md)
defines exact public/private interfaces, original reconciliation, bounds and shared custody.
Runtime activation remains unverified.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Separate deliberate saved research-policy editing
Both companions SHALL expose only the dedicated original-bound saved research-policy editor defined in contract.md, preserving absent/null/required/disabled declarations and the fixed broad inspection v1 projection.

#### Scenario: Explicit set, clear or keep
- **WHEN** the user begins an observed editor and deliberately selects required, disabled or clear
- **THEN** only research_policy is prepared/applied at most once, clear removes the key, Keep preserves null/other bytes and only the producer-bound no-change result permits skipping apply.

#### Scenario: Invalid, stale or unsupported input
- **WHEN** shape/version/size/path/identity/expiry/base/patch/target/after-view fails or dedicated producer support is unavailable
- **THEN** the operation refuses without coercion, generic-file fallback, refreshed authorization, automatic retry or changes to old inspection/editor wires.

### Requirement: Original reconciliation and shared invocation ownership
The consumer SHALL bound editor/reference lifetime and retain only the original hash-only reconciliation context after possible publication, sharing existing maintenance ownership with every then-current peer lane.

#### Scenario: Lost or uncertain publication
- **WHEN** apply may have occurred without a complete verified original response
- **THEN** the host retains that original without patch/reapply/automatic retry/eviction and permits explicit read-only desired-file checking; later refusal cannot settle the old uncertainty and desired-file observation does not prove historical authorship.

#### Scenario: Busy, Stop or stale callback
- **WHEN** another lane owns I/O, a user stops, or an old reference/completion races new work
- **THEN** Busy performs no hook/cancel/I/O, matching cancellation never becomes rollback and stale token/generation callbacks cannot cancel, release or overwrite another invocation or draft.

### Requirement: Saved-only authority and complete attributed delivery
Both companions SHALL display saved origins/policy and runtime activation unverified, preserve unrelated drafts and finish only this independently reviewed linked lifecycle under the unchanged producer contract.

#### Scenario: Verified saved policy
- **WHEN** a save verifies or an explicit original desired-file check succeeds
- **THEN** the UI reports saved-only disk state without promising running-policy adoption, restart/next-request effects, research acquisition, provider/model/native readiness or changes to per-request NoInternet priority.

#### Scenario: Propose and deliver the consumer
- **WHEN** this capability is reviewed for implementation or delivery
- **THEN** exact precode precedes source changes, synthetic source/transport and ordinary both-OS retained evidence remain separately attributed, current CI/reviews precede own sync/archive/final Docs-first delivery and actual publication is verified separately without tablet contact.
